Best Gaming Gear Under ₹1,000 in India (2026)

Want to upgrade your gaming setup without spending a fortune? Here are the things to look for when shopping for budget gaming accessories in India.

CATEGORY 01

🎯 Gaming Mouse

A good gaming mouse can make aiming and movement feel more comfortable. Look for adjustable DPI, a reliable sensor, and a shape that suits your grip.

  • Adjustable DPI
  • Reliable sensor
  • Comfortable design
  • Responsive clicks
CATEGORY 02

⌨️ Gaming Keyboard

You don't necessarily need an expensive mechanical keyboard to enjoy gaming. Prioritize responsiveness and build quality.

  • Anti-ghosting support
  • Comfortable key layout
  • Durable construction
  • Good user reviews
CATEGORY 03

🎧 Gaming Headset

Clear audio can help you hear in-game cues. Choose a headset that is comfortable for longer sessions and compatible with your PC.

  • Clear sound
  • Comfortable ear cushions
  • Decent microphone
  • 3.5mm or USB compatibility
CATEGORY 04

🖱️ Gaming Mousepad

A mousepad provides a consistent surface for your mouse. Even a budget option can be a useful addition to your setup.

  • Consistent surface
  • Non-slip rubber base
  • Stitched edges if possible

Before You Buy

Prices and availability change frequently. Always compare current prices, read recent reviews, and check compatibility before purchasing.
